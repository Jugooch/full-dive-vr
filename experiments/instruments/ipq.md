# IPQ — igroup Presence Questionnaire

- **ID:** `ipq`
- **Reference:** Schubert, T., Friedmann, F. & Regenbrecht, H. (2001). *The experience of presence: Factor analytic insights.* Presence: Teleoperators and Virtual Environments. Official items: igroup.org (IPQ).
- **Context in the research:** measures spatial presence, involvement and experienced realism ([03-diy-research-strategy](../../docs/research/03-diy-research-strategy.md)).

Item text is not reproduced here. Use the official igroup source.

## Subscales

| Subscale | Item ID prefix |
|---|---|
| General presence | `IPQ_G_` |
| Spatial presence | `IPQ_SP_` |
| Involvement | `IPQ_INV_` |
| Experienced realism | `IPQ_REAL_` |

Subscale score = mean of the items, following the official scoring instructions. Record them as `ipq_general`, `ipq_spatial`, `ipq_involvement`, `ipq_realism`.

## Response columns

`session_id,block,condition_id,item_id,response`
